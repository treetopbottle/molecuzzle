Bond every atom to find out what it is.
  1. Bonds run straight along a row or column, between atoms with nothing in between. The dotted lines show where they can go.
  2. Click a dotted line to bond. Click again for a double, then a triple bond; once more removes it. Right-click lowers it.
  3. Bonds can't cross.
  4. Each atom needs exactly its number of bonds (orange badge): H 1, O 2, N 3, C 4.
  5. All atoms join into one molecule. There is exactly one solution.
